SURVIVE THE FLOCK. KILL THE CROW KING. THEN THE TWO DARK BOSSES WAITING IN HIS CASTLE.

This is a paper manual for a live game: every kit, key and number described here is real — it documents the actual shipped build and nothing else. No feature is described that the game does not have.

The whole game runs on one canvas, in one file, with every sound synthesized at runtime — no audio assets, no sprites on disk, nothing to install. Download it and it plays offline, forever.

  • Health — hearts, filled and empty. There is no regeneration; what you lose stays lost for the run.
  • Kill count — counts up to the boss. At 10, the Crow King arrives.

  • White crows ● — passive until the Crow King screeches. Then all of them come for you at once.

02 · THE FOUR

Pick one before a run. They do not play alike — the difficulty on each is the game's own rating, printed on the character-select screen.

ARCHER — MEDIUM

The classic. Fastest shots, weakest hit; about five to kill an equal fight.

  • Bow — quiver of 10, up to 3 arrows in flight at once.
  • Dynamite — hold to charge, release to throw. Clears tiles and hurts bosses.
  • Pickups — fire arrows (leave burning patches), ricochet arrows (bounce off walls, faster each bounce).

WIZARD — EXTRA HARD

Teleguided damage and area control, with nothing to hide behind up close.

  • Magic bolts — 2s cooldown, they steer toward the nearest enemy on their own.
  • Lightning Storm — instant AoE around you; levels rock, trees and huts.
  • Pickups — fire bolt (3 dmg, homing), laser (3 dmg, passes through walls).

KNIGHT — HARD

Frontline. No answer at range at all, and the only one who does not need one.

  • Long spear — melee reach, and the thrust lands twice per swing.
  • Block — passive, no key. Banks one absorbed hit every 10 seconds, by itself.
  • Whirlwind — a spinning AoE that chews through terrain.
  • Pickups — Iron Javelin (thrown, pierces), Fire Sword (2× damage and reach, 8s).

RANGER — EASY

Skirmisher. Three chances to land a hit instead of one.

  • Crossbow — one press fires 3 independent bolts in a narrow spread, each 30% weaker than an arrow.
  • Satchel — first click throws it inert; second click arms a 3s fuse. Your own bolt sets it off early, armed or not.
  • Pickups — fire and ricochet bolts, same effects the archer gets.

THE NINE WAVES

No kill target in the castle — clearing a wave's last skeleton starts the next. Three kinds, three waves each, growing 3 → 4 → 5 within each kind, so every new threat starts light and ends heavy.

THE CASTLE GAUNTLET
WAVESKINDWHAT IT DOES
1–3NORMALWalks straight at you. One contact hit.
4–6FIRESame approach — but explodes when it dies, for 1 damage in a 50px radius. Do not stand over the kill.
7–9ICESame again, plus an ice bolt every 3s aimed where you are. A hit costs 1 and freezes you for 3 seconds — no moving, no aiming, no attacking.

FEATHERS

Kills pay out in feathers ◆, which survive the run in localStorage. Spend them from the inventory (ESC then I) on more arrows, more health, more range, more speed. They are the only thing that carries forward.

05 · THE THREE

THE CROW KING

Orbits you, charges in, screeches — and the screech turns every white crow on the map hostile at once. He summons bats on a timer while he does it.

His shield is the whole fight, and it runs on a loop:

  • 10 seconds behind a rotating blue shield — fully immune, nothing you do lands.
  • 5 seconds open. This is the window. Spend it.
  • Then a 65% chance to re-shield for another 5 (purple ring this time), up to 3 times per 30 seconds.

Kill him and the forest burns to ash around you. The castle opens.

THE DARK ARCHER & THE DARK KNIGHT

Corrupted echoes of two of your own. The archer's silhouette keeps a bow drawn on you; the knight's keeps a spear that extends on every charge. Neither carries a shield — every hit lands, from the first frame.

CASTLE BOSSES
MOVEMENTATTACKS
DARK ARCHER Orbits at range. Never closes in. Three-bolt volley · an occasional lobbed bomb that goes off in a radius · summons an ice skeleton now and then
DARK KNIGHT Short lead-in, then charges — often. Sometimes halts mid-fight into a whirlwind. A harder contact hit than the Crow King's charge · summons a fire skeleton now and then

FIRE WORKS ON ALL THREE. A fire arrow, a fire bolt or the Fire Sword ignites any boss the same way, and the burn keeps ticking after the hit lands.

06 · THE MAP

A 33×21 grid, generated fresh from a new seed every run — no two are the same, and none of them are hand-drawn.

  • Rock stops you and stops arrows. Water stops you but not arrows. Trees block both, until they burn.
  • It does not stay put. Dynamite, Lightning Storm and Whirlwind tear down rock, trees and huts permanently. The map you finish a fight on is never the one you started it on.
  • Every tree burns to ash the moment a boss arrives, opening the arena for the fight.

Two themes share that grid and every one of those rules — FOREST (boulders, trees) and CASTLE (stone walls, pillars). The castle stage always uses the second; in multiplayer the host picks either one.

07 · MULTIPLAYER

Up to four players in a room — co-op or 2v2 — on a fresh generated map every match. No boss here: a match plays to a frag target or a time limit, then everyone lands back in the lobby.

JOINING A GAME
KEYDOES

F / THost only: play to a frag target (10–30) or a time limit (5–10 min). Pick one, not both
RReady up. The match starts when the last player does

Everyone carries their own second weapon here too, all toned down from the single-player numbers — a duel is not a boss fight. Everyone also starts behind a one-hit shield that comes back when you respawn, and any hit buys you a third of a second of immunity, so a volley cannot delete you.

Every fifteen seconds or so a lone crow drifts across the arena. It dies to one hit and drops something where it falls: a fresh shield, or fire that doubles your damage for eight seconds.

IT NEEDS A SERVER. Multiplayer will not run off the downloaded file alone — someone has to host. There is a one-command Docker image and a Railway walkthrough in the README.
