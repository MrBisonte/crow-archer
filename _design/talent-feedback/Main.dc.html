what this costs in code
Pixel icons stop being Path2D and become PixelGrids, memoized in a module-level cache keyed by everything that varies — the way _skeletonGrids and _guardGrids already are — and blitted through stamps.get like every sprite. That is the same pipeline, not a new one, and it is what the ignite can then tint and bloom for free.
The bezel, the lit ground and the cast shadow are generated once from the icon’s tier colour, not drawn per icon. Thirty-seven talents therefore cost thirty-seven objects — the frame that makes them read as a set is code.
Shake stays out of it except the rite: one capstone, once a run. Shake means "something hit something" everywhere else in this game, and spending it on a menu confirmation is how that stops being true.
